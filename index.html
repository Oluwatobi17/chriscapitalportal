About Us

With today’s increasing reliance on mobile devices and data-intensive applications, the demand for robust network coverage is skyrocketing. To meet this continuous unsatiable demand, more cell sites are needed to ensure seamless connectivity everywhere. American Tower plays a pivotal role in addressing the need through our portfolio of infrastructure sites and solutions throughout the U.S.—in urban, suburban, and rural markets and everywhere in between. By working with our customers, we enable an efficient network deployment in their optimal locations, through our suite of collocation assets and solutions, including towers; rooftops; Distributed Antenna Systems (DAS), Wi-Fi, and private networks; data centers; and programs for deployment services, new tower development, and Backup Power.
